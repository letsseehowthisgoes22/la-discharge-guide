The first week after adult psychiatric discharge in Los Angeles

A hospital discharge summary may tell you what care was recommended without telling you who will make each step happen. Use this guide with the person leaving care to confirm appointments, choose a point of contact and fill practical gaps before the first weekend at home.

Where to start

Before discharge, ask the hospital team for the written plan, the first confirmed follow-up and the number to call if that appointment falls through. For Los Angeles County referrals, call 800-854-7771. A private behavioral health case manager can help coordinate several providers and track the handoff. [1] [2]

Hold a short discharge handoff

Ask the discharge planner to walk through the first day at home with the person leaving care and any support person they want involved. AHRQ's hospital-to-home guidance recommends reviewing medicines, warning signs and appointments in plain language. [1]

  • Confirm one appointmentWrite down the receiving clinic, visit date, time, location or video link and the phone number for changes. If the team only provides a referral, ask who will call to book it and when you should follow up.
  • Close the pharmacy gapReview the written medication list with the discharge team. Confirm the pharmacy, opening hours and who can answer medication questions if a prescription is delayed. Direct any questions about changing or stopping medication to the prescriber.
  • Agree on who gets updatesAsk the person leaving care whom they want involved and ask the hospital what permission each provider needs. Have the discharge packet sent through the provider's secure channel.

Find a follow-up route in Los Angeles County

Use the address where the person will stay after discharge. A hospital near Downtown LA may send someone home to the South Bay, San Gabriel Valley or San Fernando Valley. A clinic close to the discharge address or accessible by a workable ride is easier to fit into the first week.

  • Los Angeles County Help LineCall 800-854-7771 for help finding county behavioral health services. Ask for an outpatient referral near the discharge ZIP code, the intake process and how to ask about language access. [2]
  • Medi-Cal managed careWith the member's plan name and ID ready, call the plan about outpatient access. Ask whether the initial referral should go through the plan or the county mental health plan based on the person's needs and coverage. Medi-Cal has both pathways. [3]
  • A written after-hours planUse the treating team's instructions for urgent changes. For emotional or suicidal crisis support, call or text 988. Call 911 for immediate danger or a medical emergency. [4]

Decide whether to hire a private behavioral health case manager

Private case management is worth comparing when appointments are already recommended and someone still needs to connect the hospital, outpatient clinicians and home supports. Ask each candidate to explain what they would take ownership of in the first week.

  • First deliverableAsk for a written list of open tasks, a named owner and a due date for each. Ask how the coordinator tells you a referral has turned into a scheduled visit.
  • Clinical responsibilityAsk who can review medication and safety concerns. Put the coordinator's contact hours, the treating clinician's number and the after-hours plan in the same place.
  • Cost and handoffRequest the intake cost, estimated first-week scope, included meetings, charges for extra time and the cancellation terms. Ask how the coordinator hands the plan back when their work ends.

Common questions

How do I find outpatient mental health care after hospitalization in Los Angeles?

Start with the hospital's discharge planner and ask for the receiving clinic's intake contact. For county referrals, call 800-854-7771 and give the ZIP code where the person will be staying. [2]

Can I use Medi-Cal for mental health follow-up?

Medi-Cal members may receive mental health services through their managed care plan or eligible specialty care through a county mental health plan. Ask the plan or county which route applies to the recommended follow-up. [3]

What does a private case manager handle in the first week?

Depending on the written scope, a case manager can organize the handoff, confirm appointments, track practical barriers and communicate with authorized support people. The treating team handles diagnosis, medication and the clinical safety plan.

What if the first appointment is canceled?

Ask the receiving clinic for the earliest replacement and contact the discharge team about bridge follow-up. If the discharge plan lists a number for urgent issues, keep it easy to reach. Use 988 for crisis support or 911 for an emergency. [4]
